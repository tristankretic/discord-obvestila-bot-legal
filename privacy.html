Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

1. Who operates the bot

RIN Asistent (the “Bot”) is operated by the GitHub user tristankretic. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the public contact options on that GitHub profile.

2. Data we process

The Bot processes only the information needed to provide its features:

  • Discord user, server, role and channel identifiers;
  • the study-group role selected by a user;
  • deadlines entered by authorised server members, including subject, description, date and the creator’s Discord identifier;
  • server configuration, such as configured notification channels and role mappings;
  • public timetable data obtained from the configured SCNG Rapla page.

The Bot does not request passwords, payment information, private messages or the contents of ordinary server conversations.

3. Why we process data

Data is processed to assign roles, restrict access to group channels, display timetables, detect timetable changes, send relevant notifications, manage deadlines, prevent abuse and diagnose technical failures.

4. Storage and retention

Configuration, deadline information and timetable snapshots may be stored on the system running the Bot. Data is retained only while needed for the Bot’s operation or until the relevant server configuration is removed. Discord retains messages and role changes according to its own policies.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with Discord as required to operate the Bot and with SCNG Rapla when retrieving public timetable data. Their services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored data and credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may remove your group role, stop using the Bot or ask a server administrator to remove Bot-managed data. You may also contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

8. Children

The Bot is intended for members of the relevant educational community and is not directed to children under the minimum age required by Discord.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
